About Ubuntu Community Outreach Foundation

Ubuntu Community Outreach Foundation is committed to making a positive difference in the community by supporting people and promoting opportunities for growth and development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support and empower individuals and families through community outreach, educational support and development programmes.

Our Values

  • Community support
  • Compassion and respect
  • Empowerment
  • Education and development
  • Community participation

Our Commitment

We are committed to working with community members and stakeholders to identify needs and encourage activities that contribute to a stronger and more supportive community.
