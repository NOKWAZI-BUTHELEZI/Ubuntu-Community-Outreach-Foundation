Welcome to Ubuntu Community Outreach Foundation

Ubuntu Community Outreach Foundation is a community-focused organisation committed to supporting and empowering individuals and families through outreach programmes and community services.

The foundation aims to make a positive difference by promoting community development, educational support and opportunities that improve the lives of people in the community.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to encourage unity, compassion and cooperation while providing meaningful support to people who need assistance.

What We Do

  • Support community outreach activities.
  • Promote educational development and support.
  • Encourage community participation and development.
  • Empower individuals through useful programmes and services.

Get Involved

Community members, volunteers and supporters are encouraged to participate in our programmes and help us create positive change in the community.
